Our Story

A cozy corner with cold drinks

Lemon Lane & Comfort Zone started with a simple idea: students and remote workers need a pretty place to settle in that doesn't cost a fortune. We squeeze our lemonade fresh, bake our pastries daily, and keep the lights warm and soft.

Whether you're reviewing for finals, meeting a friend, or just finishing a good book, our tables stay peaceful and you're always welcome to stay as long as you like.

Our Philosophy

What drives Comfort Zone

Mission

Good drinks within reach

To serve artisanal freshly-squeezed lemonade and freshly baked pastries at honest prices, in a space where every guest feels comfortable to study, work, or unwind.

Vision

The neighborhood's favorite haven

To be the first warm retreat people think of when they want a cozy, pocket-friendly, and beautifully aesthetic space to spend their afternoon.

Values

Warmth, quality & community

To welcome everyone with a genuine smile, maintain pristine clean tables, squeeze real fruit, and keep our doors open for late study hours.
